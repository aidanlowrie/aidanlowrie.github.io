This was a focused experiment in making workflow graphs feel responsive on a
canvas. The feature set was deliberately small: blueprint-style grid, draggable
nodes, shift-click connections, pan and zoom, and a minimap.

It sits in the same family as the later workflow and graph tooling: a practical
attempt to find out what kind of interface makes structured thinking easier to
manipulate.

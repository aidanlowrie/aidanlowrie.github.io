This project started as practical speech tooling: download or ingest audio,
diarise speakers, transcribe the content, and turn the result into something
easier to inspect. Later pieces added a simple claim verifier and bulk
verification workflow.

It is messier than the course and dissertation work, but it was useful. It sits
between my speech-processing interests and the broader question of how to turn
long, messy media into structured evidence.

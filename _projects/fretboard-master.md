I built this because guitar knowledge is partly conceptual and partly motor
memory. Knowing where the notes are is not enough; you need to be able to reach
for them under pressure, hear relationships, and move between visual and aural
representations without translating slowly in your head.

The app grew into a small training environment: campaign levels, linear and
circular fretboard views, natural-note and accidental modes, blind practice,
interval drills, chord construction, chromatic-circle work, and tone training.
The interesting part is not any single screen but the attempt to make theory
practice feel like a set of repeatable exercises rather than passive reference
material.

Timestamp evidence from the local project scan places the main work between
January and March 2026, with the source living in `GuitarGame/`.

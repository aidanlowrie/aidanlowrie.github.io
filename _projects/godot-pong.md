A small Pong implementation in Godot. It is intentionally modest: ball, paddles,
collision, score, and the basic loop of making a game feel responsive.

Projects like this matter because they are fast feedback loops. You learn the
engine, the scene model, and the input/update rhythm by making something small
that either works or obviously does not.

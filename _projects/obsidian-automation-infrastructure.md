A lot of my work depends on the surrounding system: notes, search, shortcuts,
automation, and ways for language models to act on my local environment. This is
not one clean repo so much as a family of tools that accumulated around the same
need.

The strongest pieces are the Obsidian semantic-search work, the BetterTouchTool
MCP bridge, conversation tracking tools, and backups of the auto-tagger plugin.
Together they show a pattern I keep returning to: turn the personal knowledge
base into something an agent can navigate, query, and extend without destroying
the structure that made it useful in the first place.

The timestamp evidence spans May 2025 through January 2026 across
`Auto-Tagger Backups/`, `Python/BTT_MCP/`, and `MCP_Servers/`.

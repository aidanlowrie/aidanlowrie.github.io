I'm in the final stretch of an MSc in Speech and Language Processing at the University of Edinburgh. I've studied machine learning from the perspectives of vision, speech, and text, as well as its foundations.

Right now, I'm finishing my dissertation on turn-taking prediction in conversational AI. I'm investigating whether sparse autoencoders can recover the timing-related features that let humans coordinate conversation, and whether those features are causally involved in a model's predictions, or could be integrated into a model to improve turn-taking behaviour.

I have a background in biosciences, bioinformatics, and machine learning. I started with biological systems, moved into computational analysis, and have spent the past year focused on machine learning, particularly deep learning.

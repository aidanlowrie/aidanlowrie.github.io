Help with Forma

Need a hand, spotted a mistake, or have an idea?

formaquiz.support@proton.me ↗

For a bug, include your device, app version, quiz name and what happened. A screenshot helps. Beta testers can also send feedback through TestFlight.

Quick answers

Playing and practice

Choose a quiz, then a mode. Study lets you explore the answers; Learn brings questions back for more practice. In map quizzes, pinch or double-tap to zoom and drag to move around. Use the × button to leave a round.

Progress and your data

Your practice history is saved on your device. You can export it as JSON or CSV, or delete it, in Settings. Optional answer sharing has separate export and deletion controls. See the privacy policy for details.

Leaderboards

Game Center is optional. Enable sharing in Settings or Leaderboards to submit future perfect rounds. If it won’t connect, check your internet connection and Game Center sign-in in your device’s Settings. Existing Game Center scores are managed through Apple.

Quiz corrections

Send the quiz and the name of the place, structure or event, with a reliable source if possible. You can find the map editions and other sources in the app’s credits and on the content page.

Devices and offline play

Forma supports iOS and iPadOS 17 or later, and macOS 14 or later. Quizzes work offline; online features and external links need an internet connection.

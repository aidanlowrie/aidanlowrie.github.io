Privacy

Forma is developed and operated by Aidan Lowrie. This notice covers the native iPhone, iPad and Mac apps and optional answer sharing and Game Center. Updated 26 September 2026.

Your practice stays on your device

Quiz history, individual answers, accuracy, active time, pins, interests, home-country choice and preferences are stored locally. Forma does not upload these local records unless you separately choose answer sharing or leaderboard sharing as described below. Your operating system may include local files in backups according to your device settings. Forma does not provide its own cross-device sync.

Optional location suggestions

Location access is off by default. If you enable it, Apple's location services obtain a location and Apple's geocoding service determines the country. Only the country code is retained by Forma on this device. Forma does not send coordinates or the country code to its leaderboard. Location is requested when you enable or refresh suggestions; it is not monitored in the background. You can turn this off in Forma, revoke permission in system Settings, or choose a home country manually. Apple's services operate under Apple's privacy terms.

Optional Game Center

Game Center sharing is off until you choose Connect Game Center. Apple handles sign-in; Forma does not receive your Apple Account password. While sharing and practice history are enabled, Forma submits the active time of future eligible perfect rounds to the matching Apple leaderboard. Rankings are limited to supported full, untimed quizzes completed with every answer correct. Earlier local rounds are not uploaded when you connect.

Apple associates submitted results with your Game Center player profile. Forma reads player names, ranks and times to display global and friends leaderboards. Other players may see your Game Center name and results under Apple's Game Center settings and privacy terms. TestFlight results are not necessarily private to the testing group. Forma stores submission receipts on your device to avoid repeat uploads, and does not copy Game Center profiles or results to a Forma server. See Apple's Game Center privacy information.

Turn off Share future perfect rounds in Settings to stop new submissions. This does not remove results already held by Apple. Manage your Game Center profile and privacy in Apple's settings; contact Apple for requests concerning data held by Game Center. You can practise without connecting.

Sharing a quiz or result

The share button opens the system share sheet. It includes the quiz name, an app link and, for a completed round, accuracy, active time, completed count and mode. It does not include individual answers, location, player identifiers or other practice history. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and complete sharing in the selected app.

Local history and deletion

Turn off Save practice history to stop saving new answers and unfinished games and pause result sharing. Existing practice and learning history remains until deleted. Unrecognised typed answer text is used only for live feedback and is not persisted. Delete practice history removes local rounds, saved sessions, learning schedules and review logs, and clears pending research uploads; it does not delete results already shared with Apple. Export local practice as JSON or CSV in Settings. Local records remain until you delete them or remove the app's data.

Learn

Learn uses a question-order model on your device. Correct answers return after progressively larger gaps of other answered questions; mistakes return sooner. The app saves stages, answer order, correctness, response time and correction context. It does not use FSRS or calendar-based review dates. It does not diagnose learning difficulties or assign a learning-style category. Learn works without answer sharing. Local review detail is limited to the most recent 10,000 Learn answers and 1,000 answers per saved session; summary measurements and earned stages are retained. Earlier FSRS logs remain available for export until you delete practice history, but no longer influence scheduling.

Optional answer sharing — all quiz modes

Sharing is off until you explicitly agree. In this beta only adults aged 18 or older may opt in; everyone can use the quizzes without sharing. You are asked once when starting a quiz and can change your choice in Settings. This choice is separate from notifications, location and Game Center. It covers future answers across ordinary quizzes and Learn, not only Learn. Previously saved history is not uploaded.

If you agree, Aidan Lowrie collects a random participant identifier and answer records to evaluate and improve Forma's question selection and practice algorithms. Each record includes a research-only session/event identifier, quiz and target content identifiers, recognised selected content identifier or selected timeline year, mode, question position, correctness, response duration, timeout and typo flags, correction context, filled/available counts, Learn question gap/early-review status and algorithm/app version. The review day is recorded rather than a precise answer timestamp. Unknown typed text is not included. Names, email addresses, contacts, location, home-country choices, advertising identifiers and Game Center identities are not included in this dataset. Research identifiers are separate from public leaderboard identifiers.

The persistent random identifier links your sessions, so these records are pseudonymous personal data, not anonymous. They are used only for research: internal statistical analysis of how people learn and forget, and fitting/testing Forma's learning algorithms. They are not sold, published as participant-level datasets, used for advertising or sent to a third-party AI model service. Individual adaptation runs locally. Any future materially different use requires an updated explanation and appropriate permission.

Answer sharing runs on Aidan Lowrie's own Cloudflare account (Cloudflare Workers and D1), with the answer database held under Cloudflare's EU jurisdiction, so it is stored in the European Union. Cloudflare processes this data on our behalf under its customer data processing addendum. Requests necessarily expose a network address and request metadata to Cloudflare's infrastructure. Our application does not log answer bodies or credentials, and request logging is turned off. Cloudflare may retain security logs and backups under its own security, recovery and deletion procedures; we do not promise immediate removal from those copies. Records shared under the earlier 12-month consent were held by the original OpenAI Sites service; updating Forma deletes them there. See Cloudflare's data processing addendum and D1 data location.

Because they are used only for research, answer records shared with this version of the consent are kept indefinitely, until you withdraw and request deletion. Learning is studied over long periods, so there is no fixed end date. Records shared under the earlier 12-month consent keep that limit; updating the app deletes those records and asks you again. Any row-level analysis copies follow the same deletion requests. No fitted population model has yet been trained or published from these records.

Turning sharing off immediately stops new collection on this device, clears its pending queue and requests authenticated deletion of the participant and answer records from the active database. Offline deletion remains visibly pending and retries when connected; keep Forma installed until confirmation. Consent withdrawals must also be applied to operator-held row-level analysis copies. A later opt-in creates a new identity. Turning off practice history pauses uploads and clears the queue, but does not itself delete previously shared records. Deleting local history also clears the queue; use the separate shared-data deletion control to delete server records.

The offline queue holds up to 1,000 events, with unsent records older than 29 days discarded. Credentials are stored in the device Keychain; the server stores a credential hash. You can export your shared records or withdraw in Settings. Support can help with access/deletion if you lose the device: quote your research ID, but never email a credential. We may need to establish that a request concerns your records before disclosing data. We cannot identify a random profile from a personal name alone.

Consent is the basis for this optional answer sharing. Refusing or withdrawing does not reduce access to Learn or other quizzes. Necessary hosting/security processing protects the service. Withdrawal does not invalidate earlier lawful processing.

Support, sources and tracking

If you email support, we receive your email address and the message and attachments you choose to send, and use them to answer your request. Avoid including sensitive information. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to resolve and document the request and meet applicable obligations. We do not sell personal data, include advertising SDKs, or track users across other companies' apps or websites. Opening source links or email uses your chosen browser or email app and the relevant provider's policies.

Contact and your rights

Contact Aidan Lowrie at formaquiz.support@proton.me for support, privacy questions, access, correction or deletion requests. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your data protection authority. Optional public sharing and location are based on your choice; we use necessary service/security processing to provide and protect the service. Withdrawing optional consent does not affect earlier lawful processing. Hosting may process data internationally under its applicable safeguards. Material changes to this notice will be reflected here and in the published policy.

This page summarises the core academic strands from my AUC notes, where I
combined molecular and cellular biology with neurobehavioural and ethical
analysis.

## Core academic strands

| Module | Main focus | What I built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brain and Cognition | Neural communication, emotion, multifactorial mental conditions, neurobiology of behaviour, psychiatric framing, neurodiversity topics. | Linking neurobiology to behaviour, and evidence to societal interpretation. |
| Molecular Cell Biology | DNA replication/repair, membranes/transport, intracellular compartments, signalling, cell cycle, mitochondria, stem-cell/cancer contexts. | Multi-scale cell-biology reasoning and model-system comparison. |
| Immunology | Innate/adaptive immunity, B/T development, antigen presentation, mucosal immunity, vaccination, autoimmunity, cancer and immune failure. | Mechanism-to-disease translation and case/proposal-style scientific synthesis. |
| Bioethics | Care ethics, reproductive ethics, end-of-life ethics, incidental genetic findings, identity and decision-making. | Structured ethical argument under applied healthcare scenarios. |

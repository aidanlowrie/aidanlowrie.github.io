## Course objectives captured in notes

### Brain and Cognition

Notes capture objectives around:

- Structure/function of the nervous system and neural communication.
- Genetic and environmental contributions to multifactorial conditions.
- Neurobiological research translation to brain health and well-being.
- Critical reflection on boundaries of normal/abnormal behaviour and social
  implications.
- Literature search + scientific opinion writing.

### Immunology

Notes capture objectives around:

- Principles of innate/adaptive immunity in human well-being context.
- Tissue-specific immune requirements.
- Immune failures (infection, allergy, autoimmunity, cancer).
- Medication and immunotherapy as repair strategies.
